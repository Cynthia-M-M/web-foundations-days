About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight, distraction-free tool designed for engineers and creators to jot down sudden sparks of inspiration. Whether you are mapping out a new database architecture or simply listing daily tasks, this app keeps your thoughts organized.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Click on the "New note" input field on the Home page.
  2. Type your idea, task, or snippet into the text box.
  3. Press Enter on your keyboard or click the "Add Note" button to save it to your list.

Features

  • Lightning-fast text entry
  • Clean, semantic HTML5 structure
  • Accessible forms for screen readers
  • Responsive, distraction-free design

Keyboard shortcuts
